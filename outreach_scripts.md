# Pentagon Studios — Outreach DM Scripts

Copy-paste templates for messaging artist leads. Swap the [brackets]. Voice =
casual, confident, Atlanta. Value first, pitch last. One ask per message.

## The flow

### 1 · Opener — the LUFS hook (strongest; you already listened)
Sandwich the technical finding with a specific compliment so it doesn't read
like a corporate audit.
> Yo [Name], caught [Song Name] on the monitors today. The vocal chain you're using is crazy, but I noticed the master is pushing around -7 LUFS and clipping a bit on the low end. Who's engineering your stuff right now?

### 1b · Opener — no analysis yet (ATL local angle)
Lean into the local-scene angle to create a curiosity hook.
> Yo [Name], came across your page while looking for new ATL talent. [Insert genuine compliment]. Are you recording out of a home setup right now or working out of a studio?

### 2 · The Reply Handler (the pivot)
Frame the free sample as a "test drive" — position yourself as in-demand, not
desperate.
> Yeah, mastering trap and R&B can be tricky with those heavy low-ends. Tell you what — send me the stems for one track. I'll mix and master a 45-second snippet for free just so you can hear the difference. If you mess with it, we can talk about a full project.

### 3 · The Warm Close
Let the free mix do the selling. Point to the EPK/one-pager without talking
money yet.
> Here's the bounce. Let me know how it sounds in your car. If you want to lock in the rest of the track, here is my link with the breakdown.

### 4 · The No-Reply Follow-Up
Brutally short, give them an out to remove sales pressure.
> Yo [Name], just bumping this. No stress if you're locked in on other projects right now, just wanted to leave the door open. Keep grinding.

## Strategic note — Management Pivot

If the artist's audio is already top-tier but their branding/booking presence
is messy, swap the LUFS hook for an **Artist Management hook** pointing to the
$400 Artist Development Package instead of studio services:
> Your sound is undeniable, but your release rollout could be pushing way more numbers.

## Principles (freestyle off these)
- Lead with value, never the pitch. First message gives something; never asks for money.
- Be specific. Name the song. Name the problem. Generic = ignored.
- One ask per message. Each DM ends with a single easy yes.
- The free sample is the closer, not the price. Once they hear their song leveled up, $150 is nothing.
- Never sound thirsty. "Whenever you want it" beats "you in??" — you're the pro choosing them.

## Pricing (for reference when they ask)
- Single (mix + master): $150
- 3-song bundle: $400 (~$133/song) — the best-value anchor, push this
- EP / 5 songs: $650 (~$130/song)
- Mastering only: $60/song
- Always offer the free track analysis + free sample mix first to de-risk.
- Artist Development Package (management): $400 upfront (60 days) + 15% commission after. Never below $400 total.
